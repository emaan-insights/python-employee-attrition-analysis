# Employee Attrition Analysis using Python

## Project Overview
This project explores employee attrition patterns using Python and the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset. The goal is to identify workforce trends and understand which factors are associated with employees leaving an organization.

## Objectives
- Examine overall employee attrition.
- Compare attrition rates across departments and job roles.
- Analyze the relationship between overtime and attrition.
- Compare income, job satisfaction, age, and company tenure.
- Explore work-life balance differences between employees who stayed and left.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook
- VS Code

## Key Findings
- Overall employee attrition was approximately 16.12%.
- Sales had the highest departmental attrition rate at 20.63%.
- Employees working overtime had a higher attrition rate (30.53%) than those not working overtime (10.44%).
- Employees who left had lower average monthly income.
- Employees who stayed had higher average job satisfaction.
- Sales Representatives had the highest job-role attrition rate at 39.76%.
- Employees who stayed had longer average company tenure.
